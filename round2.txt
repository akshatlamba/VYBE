06 / RECOMMENDATION LOGIC
How matching stays explainable
First remove opportunities the student cannot apply for. Then rank the remaining options with visible reasons.
1 HARD ELIGIBILITY
Academic year • department • location • deadline • mandatory
criteria. Ineligible opportunities are excluded.
2 WEIGHTED FIT
Illustrative weights: interests 30% • goals 25% • budget 15% •
schedule 15% • format 10% • level 5%.
3 READINESS
Prerequisites met • skills to strengthen • time to prepare • checklist
completion.
EXAMPLE OUTPUT
86/100 fit | Eligible | Readiness: 3 of 4 preparation items complete.
VALIDATION NOTE
Weights are configurable design assumptions, not validated
performance claims. Tune them through pilot feedback.
Proposed design. Prototype scope and performance claims will be validated during implementation.

08 / RESPONSIBLE DESIGN
Trust, verification & privacy
Trust is part of the product architecture—not a final checklist.
SOURCE TRACEABILITY
Store source URL, organizer, last-checked time and listing status.
Flag stale details.
HONEST ACHIEVEMENTS
Separate self-reported, evidence-attached and organizer-verified
outcomes. Never label all as verified.
STUDENT CONTROL
Let students edit profile details, choose what to share and remove
optional evidence.
ROLE-BASED ACCESS
Separate student, institution staff and admin permissions;
institutional views are aggregated.
SAFE AI BOUNDARIES
AI may summarize or suggest. Eligibility, ranking and status
changes remain rule-checked and reviewable.
Proposed design. Prototype scope and performance claims will be validated during implementation.

11 / VALIDATION
How we will test whether it works
Measure decision quality and workflow completion—not just page views.
MATCH QUALITY Review top recommendations against a labelled sample with students or mentors.
ELIGIBILITY ACCURACY Check whether displayed opportunities pass mandatory criteria.
EXPLANATION CLARITY Test whether users can explain why an opportunity was recommended.
TASK COMPLETION Measure checklist completion and application-stage updates.
DATA FRESHNESS Track active listings with a recent source check and valid deadline.
RISKS & MITIGATIONS Stale listings → source checks and expiry. Cold start → short onboarding. False verification → explicit evidence states. Biased ranking → visible reasons and user overrides.
NO UNPROVEN CLAIMS Proposed design. Prototype scope and performance claims will be validated during implementation.
Set numeric targets after a pilot baseline; claim no impact or accuracy result before testing.
